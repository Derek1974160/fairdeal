Step one of your application

Get the exact documents your Fair Deal application needs

Six short questions. You get a checklist built for your situation, the warnings that matter, and a copy by email if you want one. No account, no payment.

Who is this for?

Everything here is about the person who is going into care. We call them the applicant.

Who is filling this in?
Can the applicant understand and sign the application?
Is there an enduring power of attorney?

Their situation

Is the applicant one of a couple?

Married, civil partners, or living together as a couple for three years or more.

Where are they right now?

Income

Tick every source of income the applicant has. For a couple, tick the partner's too.

Savings and investments

Tick everything held, even small amounts. The HSE wants a statement for each.

Has the applicant given away money or property in the last five years?

Gifts to children, paying off someone's mortgage, signing over a house or land.

Property

Does the applicant own their home, or a share of it?
Is there a mortgage or loan secured on it?
Who will live in the house once the applicant is in care?
Would you like to defer the property part of the contribution until after death (the nursing home loan)?
Does the applicant own any of these?

Almost there

Two last things, then your checklist.

Has a nursing home been chosen?
Is the applicant's spouse or partner also likely to need care soon?
