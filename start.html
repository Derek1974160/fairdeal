Fair Deal help from your nursing home

Type the code on the sheet the home gave you.

Ask to set you up with Fair Deal help

It costs your family nothing. The home presses Approve, usually the same day, and you receive a private link by email or text to start. The service works out the likely weekly cost, fills in the official HSE form from plain questions, keeps a checklist of every document, sends the request letters for you, and reminds you what to do next.

What the home can see: how far along the application is. What it cannot see: your income, savings or PPS numbers. Those stay on your own phone or computer. Privacy notice.
