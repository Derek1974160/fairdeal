How to read the price

Every private and voluntary nursing home in Fair Deal has a maximum weekly price agreed with the National Treatment Purchase Fund. Public homes run by the HSE publish a weekly cost of care instead. Both are the full weekly cost before any State support.

  • The price does not change what you pay. Your contribution is fixed by the financial assessment (80% of income and 7.5% of assets). The HSE pays the rest, whether the home charges €1,200 or €2,200. See your figure.
  • Extras are not included. Hairdressing, therapies, activities, newspapers, transport to appointments and some incontinence products can be charged separately. Ask every home for its list of extra charges in writing before you sign the contract of care.
  • A higher price is not a higher standard. Prices reflect location and buildings more than care. Read the inspection reports.
  • Public homes can cost more on paper because their figure is a cost of care rather than a negotiated price. Your contribution is the same either way.

What to look for in an inspection report

HIQA inspects every nursing home and publishes the report. Each one grades the home against the regulations. Look at the most recent report and the one before it for:

  • Staffing levels and whether residents said they wait long for help
  • Any regulation marked not compliant, and whether the same one appears in the previous report
  • Complaints handling and how residents’ rights were described
  • Fire safety and premises findings, which are the most common failures
  • The provider’s response: specific dates to fix things, or vague promises

Then visit at a mealtime, unannounced if the home allows it, and talk to a resident’s family in the car park.
