Set up a family

Send a family their link

The family gets an email from fairdeal.ie with your home’s name on it. They answer a few questions and receive the full service: the step-by-step pack, the official form filled in, the checklist with reminders, and the request letters.

Please do not fill in the form for the family on this computer: their PPS numbers and finances should stay on their own device.

Covering the cost

How does your home cover it?

For your own records. Some homes include it in their admission arrangements; others pass it on to the family as a clearly itemised charge agreed before admission. That is your decision, with your own advice.
