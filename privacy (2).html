Privacy notice

How fairdeal.ie uses your information

Version 3, 10 October 2026.

Who we are

fairdeal.ie is run by Alderidge Fusion Limited, a company registered in Ireland (company number 816930), registered office 27 Castlesize Drive, Sallins, Co. Kildare, W91 Y9E8. We decide how your information is used, so we are the “controller” under data protection law.

To contact us about your information, email derek@legacysteps.ie or phone 087 759 9848.

fairdeal.ie is an independent service. It is not run by, connected to, or approved by the HSE.

The short version

  • The calculator works on your own device. Nothing you type into it is sent to us.
  • When you fill in the official HSE form or a request letter with us, your answers stay on your own phone or computer. PPS numbers, bank balances and income figures are never sent to us.
  • If you buy a service, we keep your email address, the first name you give, your answers to the checklist questions and your progress, so we can provide the service.
  • We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

What we collect, and why

The calculator

The calculator runs in your browser. The figures you enter are not sent to us. If you ask us to tell you when the service opens, we keep your email address so we can do that.

The free checklist

If you ask us to email you the free document checklist, we keep your email address, the first name you give, your answers to the checklist questions and the checklist itself. We use them to send you the checklist, and we may email you once about our paid service. We receive a copy of the request so we can help if you reply.

Buying a service

When you buy “Form only”, “Full service” or the upgrade, we keep:

  • your email address and the first name you give;
  • your answers to the checklist questions, for example whether the applicant is married, owns a home, or is able to sign the form;
  • a record of the payment: what you bought, the amount and the date;
  • a record that you ticked the box at checkout, with the date and the wording you agreed to.

Some answers can reveal information about the applicant’s health, such as whether they are able to sign the form, or whether they are in hospital or a nursing home. We use these answers only to prepare your pack, checklist and reminders, and only because you agree to it when you tick the box at checkout.

Card payments are handled by Stripe. We never see or store your card number.

Your tracker (Full service)

Your tracker keeps your checklist, which items you have ticked, the dates you mark (for example when you posted the form), the support office area you choose, and a log of the emails we send you. We use these to show your progress and to send your reminders.

Your tracker is opened with a private link. We store only a scrambled version of the link, so we cannot recreate it.

What stays on your own device

When you fill in the official HSE form or a request letter on fairdeal.ie, what you type is kept in your browser on your own phone or computer, and the completed form is created there. It is not sent to us. We only record that a form was downloaded, not what was in it. You can remove your answers at any time with “Clear my answers from this device”. Clearing your browser’s data also removes them.

If a nursing home arranged the service for your family

Some nursing homes pay for fairdeal.ie for their residents’ families. If yours did, the invitation came from us with the home’s name on it, and you were asked to agree before starting.

The nursing home can see the resident’s first name, the stage the application has reached (gathering documents, posted or approved), how many documents are ticked off, and the dates. It cannot see your answers to the checklist questions, your letters, or anything you type into the HSE form. Income, savings and PPS numbers stay on your own device.

We keep a record that the home arranged the service, for billing, for six years.

Nursing home staff

For staff who use the nursing home pages, we keep your name, work email and role, the home you work for, and a record of the families you sent a link to. We use these to let you sign in and to bill the home. Sign-in codes are deleted after a day. If staff dictate or type admission notes to fill in the set-up form, the notes are read once to pick out the fields and the checklist answers and are not stored; the answers travel with the family’s invitation so the family can check them, and are deleted with the invitation if it is never used.

Our legal reasons for using your information

  • To provide the service you bought (contract): your email, name, answers, tracker and reminders.
  • Your explicit consent: for answers that may reveal information about health. You can withdraw it by emailing us, although we will then not be able to continue the service.
  • Legal obligation: keeping payment records for six years for tax purposes.
  • Legitimate interests: keeping the service secure, and replying to your questions, including through the automated “Ask a question” guide. Questions and answers are kept for 12 months to improve the guidance, and a question the guide cannot answer is emailed to us so a person can reply.
  • Your consent: the calculator sign-up and the one email about our service after the free checklist. You can ask us to stop at any time.

Who else handles your information

We use these companies to run fairdeal.ie. They act on our instructions and may not use your information for their own purposes.

  • Supabase stores our database, in a data centre in Ireland.
  • Vercel hosts the website pages.
  • Resend sends our emails.
  • Stripe processes card payments.
  • Twilio sends text messages, when a nursing home sends a family their link by text.
  • Anthropic provides the automated answers for “Ask a question” and reads nursing home staff’s dictated notes to fill in the set-up form. It receives the question or the notes, and, for a question, the progress information shown on your own screen. It does not keep them or use them to train its systems.

Speaking instead of typing

Where you see a microphone button, your browser turns your voice into text. That is done by your browser’s own speech service (for example Google’s on Chrome and Android, Apple’s on iPhone and Safari), under that company’s privacy terms, and the text appears on your screen for you to check. On the HSE form, the words are sorted into the boxes on your own device; nothing you say there is sent to us. For “Ask a question”, the text of your question is sent to us only when you press Ask.

The website also loads fonts from Google Fonts, and the form-filling tool from jsDelivr. When your browser does this, those services see your device’s internet address, as with any website.

Some of these companies are based in the United States. Where information is transferred outside the European Economic Area, they rely on the safeguards allowed by data protection law, such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.

We do not share your information with the HSE. You send your application to the HSE yourself. The only other organisation that can see anything is a nursing home that arranged the service for your family, as set out below.

How long we keep it

  • Payments not completed: deleted after 30 days.
  • Free checklist requests: deleted after 12 months.
  • Calculator sign-ups: deleted after 24 months.
  • Your answers on a paid order: removed 12 months after payment. The payment record itself (email, what you bought, amount and date) is kept for six years for tax purposes.
  • Your pack (PDF): kept for 12 months so we can resend it if you lose it, then deleted.
  • Your tracker: deleted six months after it is closed, or after three years with no activity.
  • Billing records for a nursing home that arranged the service: six years.
  • Copies of emails we send you: deleted after 12 months.

These deletions happen automatically each night.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your information, to correct it, to delete it, to limit how we use it, or to object to how we use it. You can also ask for a copy in a form you can take elsewhere. Email derek@legacysteps.ie. We will reply within one month.

To stop our reminder emails, reply STOP to any of them.

If you are not happy with how we have handled your information, you can complain to the Data Protection Commission at dataprotection.ie.

Applying for someone else

Most families use fairdeal.ie on behalf of a parent or relative. Please only give us information about the applicant that you are entitled to share, and let them know you are using the service where you can.

Changes to this notice

If we change how we use your information, we will update this page and change the version date at the top.
